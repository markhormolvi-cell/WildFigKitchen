Our Story

Welcome to
Wild Fig Kitchen

Located in Coarsegold, CA, Wild Fig Kitchen is a family restaurant dedicated to providing fresh food from the finest available ingredients.

Wild Fig Kitchen opened in March 2017 and is located in the Historical Coarsegold Village in Coarsegold, CA. Since then, chef and owner Marc Neff has been serving fresh and original interpretations of breakfast and lunch favorites.

In the Fall of 1998, Marc and his wife Jacque moved their small family from the beaches of Southern California to the Foothills of the Sierra Nevada. Since the move, it has long been Marc's dream to create a mountain restaurant that caters to the hungry traveller, the outdoor enthusiast, Yosemite Weekender, and mountain folk alike. So, whether you live down the street or are just passing through, we invite you to join us for breakfast or lunch.
